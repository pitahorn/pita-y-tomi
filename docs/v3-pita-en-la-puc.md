# v3 — Pita y Tomi en la PUC

Agreed with Pita via /grilling and play-testing, 2026-10-13. Second minigame,
same shape as the Desafío 42K. Front-end only.

## Entry
- Floating **Pita head** on the landing, next to Tomi's. Same "only after the
  story" rule. Own link: `matri.pitahorn.com/puc`.
- Menu "Pita y Tomi en la PUC", FÁCIL / DIFÍCIL, on the game's own paused scene.

## Play
- In front of the Escuela de Ingeniería. Pita balances the stack on her head and
  follows the finger (desktop: ← →).
- Coursework lands where it hits the top. A piece hanging past its support slides
  off alone. Past the tipping point the tower shakes with a "!" and the **next
  catch** decides: counterweighted back, it's saved; still past, it falls.
- Fall: "Todo se derrumbó 💔", books on a pile, Pita asleep on top (eyes closed).
- 12 semesters; a semester moves up after N catches (Fácil 3, Difícil 4).
- Difícil: faster falls each semester, giant drops at 6 and 12, the calculator.
- Camera: from semester 9 it slides down to give the tower room.

## Tiredness
- Energy drains faster with a taller tower; snacks (café, muffin, galleta,
  almohada) refill it, caught by Pita or any part of the tower.
- Face in 4 steps: small bags, big bags, wiry hair, very wiry hair. The vertical
  bar on the right shows the same face.
- At 0%: "Ya me cansé 🎵". The next catch decides: food wakes her, a book drops it all.

## Besito de la suerte
- Fácil: 2 (semesters 4–6 and 8–11). Difícil: 1 (7–11).
- Straightens the tower halfway, stops the sway, saves an unstable tower. Tomi
  walks in for a kiss (2.4 s pause).

## Fácil vs Difícil
- Fácil: steady falls, half the sway, wider tipping margin, slower drain, hints.

## Results
- Win: tower bursts into papers, Pita and Tomi in ceremony clothes hop, confetti
  and diplomas. "¡Titulada!". Buttons: Compartir · ↻ Otra vez · ☰ Menú · Salir.
- Topple: "TODO SE DERRUMBÓ 💔 · Semestre N". Buttons: ↻ Otra vez · ☰ Menú · Salir.
